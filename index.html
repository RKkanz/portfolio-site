Selected work

Six projects across humanoids, marine autonomy, aerial systems and assistive robotics, plus a side project.

Foundation Robotics · 2026 — Present

Real-time teleoperation smart gloves

Lead engineer for the gloves operators use to drive a humanoid's hands. I own the hardware–software integration for robot hand control. I raised the hand-tracking update rate from 50 Hz to 200 Hz, chose an IMU-based architecture mixing 6-DOF and 9-DOF IMUs to keep the electronics small on the back of the hand, and prototyped a Hall-effect exoskeleton glove reaching 0.5° per-joint accuracy. A data-collection glove with palm and fingertip tactile sensing was working within a month.

  • Teleoperation
  • Sensor integration
  • Real-time control

ASNE · Aug 2025 — Jan 2026

Autonomous boat, 40 knots

Owned the full autonomy and AI stack (ROS + ArduPilot), including all onboard control systems and navigation policies, and filtered GPS, IMU and vision noise for real-time state estimation. I also laid up the fiberglass planing V-hull, which beat prior displacement hulls to reach 40 knots, and designed the power system with battery integration and thermal management.

  • ROS
  • ArduPilot
  • Sensor filtering
  • Composites

UIUC Human Dynamics & Control Lab · Aug 2025 — Jan 2026

Parkinsonian training robot

Robotic arm and leg modules that reproduce Parkinsonian tremor (4–6 Hz) and jerk-like motion for clinical training. A redesign of the electromechanical systems cut motion error by about 30%, and enclosing moving mechanisms with TPU and silicone bumpers removed pinch points.

  • Mechatronics
  • Clinical
  • Motion fidelity

Aerial Industries · May — Aug 2024

12-motor UAV chassis

Led the production release of a 12-motor UAV chassis, taking it from prototype to flight-ready hardware. I built parametric CAD for the propulsion and payload systems, integrating structural, thermal and electrical requirements.

  • Production release
  • Parametric CAD
  • Propulsion

Aerial Mechanica · Apr — Aug 2025

Patented motor design

Owned a patented motor design end to end, from first concept through production release. I also used FEA and risk analysis to validate the carbon-fiber frame and landing legs under dynamic flight loads.

  • Electromechanical
  • FEA
  • Carbon fiber

UIUC Human Dynamics & Control Lab · Aug 2024 — Aug 2025

Assistive torso-driven backrest

A hybrid sensing system (rotary encoders + time-of-flight) that estimates torso angle with under 2° error for real-time control. It sits on a compliant backrest with mechanical angle limits, so it can safely "hug" the user, and the multi-sensor hardware reached over 90% signal consistency.

  • Sensing
  • Compliant mechanisms
  • HRI
