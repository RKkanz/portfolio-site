Selected work

Six projects across humanoids, marine autonomy, aerial systems and assistive robotics.

Foundation Robotics · 2026 — Present

Real-time teleoperation smart gloves

Lead engineer for the gloves operators use to drive a humanoid's hands. I own the hardware–software integration, from sensing on the glove to joint commands on the robot hand, with latency low enough to feel direct.

  • Teleoperation
  • Sensor integration
  • Real-time control

ASNE · 2025 — 2026

Autonomous boat, 40 knots

Owned the full autonomy stack (ROS + ArduPilot) and filtered GPS, IMU and vision noise for real-time state estimation. I also laid up the fiberglass planing hull and designed the power and thermal system.

  • ROS
  • ArduPilot
  • Sensor fusion
  • Composites

UIUC Human Dynamics & Control Lab · 2025

Parkinsonian training robot

Robotic arm and leg modules that reproduce 4–6 Hz tremor and jerk-like rigidity so clinicians can practise on them. A redesign of the electromechanical system cut motion error by about 30%.

  • Mechatronics
  • Clinical
  • Motion fidelity

Aerial Industries · 2024

12-motor UAV chassis

Took a heavy-lift multirotor from prototype to flight-ready production hardware. I built parametric CAD for the propulsion and payload systems, balancing structural, thermal and electrical requirements.

  • Production release
  • Parametric CAD
  • Propulsion

Aerial Mechanica · 2025

Patented motor design

Owned a new motor design end to end, from first concept through production release. I also used FEA and risk analysis to validate the carbon-fiber frame and landing legs under dynamic flight loads.

  • Electromechanical
  • FEA
  • Carbon fiber

UIUC Human Dynamics & Control Lab · 2024 — 2025

Assistive torso-driven backrest

A hybrid sensing system (rotary encoders + time-of-flight) that estimates torso angle to within 2° for hands-free control. It sits on a compliant backrest with hard angle limits, so it can safely "hug" the user.

  • Sensing
  • Compliant mechanisms
  • HRI
